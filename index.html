ChequeSystem for Mac

ChequeSystem delivers a complete cheque printing and management solution for macOS. Streamline issuing with precise, professional cheques—payee, date, amount in text and numbers, plus secure features like "A/C Payee Only" and "Non-Negotiable". Supports laser, inkjet, and dot-matrix printers for crisp results. Effortless batch and duplex printing, full historical records, recurring templates, and customizable layouts from over 200 bank formats.

Effortless Printing

Print cheques with all essentials in seconds. Batch process accelerates high-volume needs, while duplex adds back-side details. Compatible with any printer for professional, legible output every time.

Smart Management

Track every issued cheque with notes, audit codes, and organized payee lists for one-click entry. Recurring templates auto-generate repeats; batch creation handles multiples efficiently. Manage multiple chequebooks and companies seamlessly.

Powerful Reporting

Generate Account Reports, Payee Reports, and Monthly Statements. Export to PDF or Excel for analysis and sharing. Gain clear insights into your cheque activity without complexity.

Fully Customizable

Adapt to your needs: select from 200+ bank layouts or design custom ones with the intuitive tool. Set currency, numbering, and formats precisely. Version 8.7.0 brings refined themes, UX enhancements, and macOS-optimized interfaces.

How It Works

Step 1: Set Up

Launch ChequeSystem and configure your chequebook, company, and printer. Select from built-in layouts or design your own.

Step 2: Create Cheques

Enter payee details with one-click from lists, add amounts, dates, and notes. Use templates for recurring or batch multiple cheques.

Step 3: Print & Track

Preview, print via batch or duplex. All cheques log automatically with audit trails for full history.

Step 4: Report & Export

View reports on accounts, payees, or months. Export to PDF/Excel for records or sharing.
